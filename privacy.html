This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use smooz-tech.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and specific to what this website actually does.

In this policy, "Smooz Technologies", "we" and "us" mean this company. We are responsible for the personal data described below. For any privacy question or request, write to gc@smooz-tech.com.

2. What we collect

  • Contact form. The name (optional), email address, subject and message you type into the form, plus anything else you choose to include.
  • Email. If you write to us by email, we keep your message and your email address.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the services that deliver this site (see section 4) process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this website, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if relevant, to discuss advisory work or our products.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Service providers we rely on

We use the following providers to run the website. Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

  • Formspree — receives and forwards contact form submissions.
  • Microsoft — hosts the email service for our domain.
  • GitHub — hosts the website files.
  • Google Fonts and unpkg — deliver fonts and a script used by the contact form; your browser connects to them when a page loads, so they can see your IP address.

These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies. We may add or replace providers over time and will keep this list up to date.

5. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, including outside Lebanon. By using the contact form or email, you understand that your data may be processed in those countries.

6. How long we keep data

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and to maintain reasonable business records, then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see section 7).

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Lebanese law on personal data, and, where it applies to you, other laws such as the GDPR, you may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is inaccurate;
  • delete your data;
  • stop using your data for a particular purpose, or withdraw consent you gave.

Write to gc@smooz-tech.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

8. Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Third-party services described in section 4 may process technical data when you load a page or submit a form.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, including relying on providers that use encryption in transit. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

This website is intended for businesses and professionals and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

12. Contact

Questions or complaints about how we handle personal data: gc@smooz-tech.com.
